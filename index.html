Started as a hobby, never grew out of it

Hardware Lab

I've been taking things apart and rewiring them since high school. At some point it stopped being just a hobby — now I build these for my own house, and occasionally for a client who wants the same. Everything here is ESP32/Arduino at the core, WiFi-enabled, and controllable from a browser.

Smart Tank Manager

Senses water level, runs the pump, cuts it off before it ever runs dry — with a 7" dashboard and remote monitoring from anywhere.

Smart Outdoor Light

Follows sunrise and sunset on its own, wakes up for movement at night, and takes a manual override when you need one.

Desktop Ambient Light

Reads the colours off my monitor in real time and throws them onto the wall behind it — plus a manual mode with a few billion other options.

5.1 Home Theatre, Auto-Tuned

A 500W setup with a microcontroller doing the room correction — it listens to the space and adjusts tone and volume live, no manual EQ-ing.

I also take on school and college electronics projects, if you're stuck on one — reasonably priced, properly built.
