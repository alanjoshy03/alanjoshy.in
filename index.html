Started as a hobby, never grew out of it

Hardware Lab

I've been taking things apart and rewiring them since high school. At some point it stopped being just a hobby — now I build these for my own house, and occasionally for a client who wants the same. Everything here is ESP32/Arduino at the core, WiFi-enabled, and controllable from a browser.

Smart Tank Manager

Senses water level, runs the pump, cuts it off before it ever runs dry — with a 7" dashboard and remote monitoring from anywhere.

Smart Outdoor Light

Follows sunrise and sunset on its own, wakes up for movement at night, and takes a manual override when you need one.

Desktop Ambient Light

Reads the colours off my monitor in real time and throws them onto the wall behind it — plus a manual mode with a few billion other options.

5.1 Home Theatre, Auto-Tuned

A 500W setup with a microcontroller doing the room correction — it listens to the space and adjusts tone and volume live, no manual EQ-ing.

I also take on school and college electronics projects, if you're stuck on one — reasonably priced, properly built.

Where the Xpulse has taken me

Journal

Most weekends that aren't spoken for go to offroading, camping, or a long ride with no fixed plan — usually with the same three friends. Work's had me mostly grounded lately, so a few of these are still being written up properly, but here's where the Xpulse has been.

Last year

Meesapulimala, with the usual four

The main trip of the year — myself and three friends, up to one of the highest peaks in the Western Ghats.

Trek

Chokramudi Trek

A steep climb near Munnar with views that make the knee pain the next day feel worth it.

Camping

Vagamon, a tent in the middle of nowhere

No signal, no plan past "pitch the tent before dark" — exactly the kind of weekend that resets everything.

Wildlife

Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary

Slower pace, quieter roads — a good reminder that not every ride needs to be about the distance covered.

Road trip

Vattavada

One of the furthest, quietest corners of Kerala — carrot fields, cold mornings, and a long ride back.

Currently: heads-down at work, trail log paused. Older trips and new ones will land here as time allows.
